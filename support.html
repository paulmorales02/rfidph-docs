Support - RFID PH

RFID PH lets you check your Autosweep and Easytrip RFID toll balances in one place, without logging into two separate provider websites.

Need help or found a bug?

Email us at PMSoft.ph@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Why does adding an account take a while?
The app looks up your balance directly from the provider's own site in real time, so it can take a few seconds depending on how fast that provider's server responds.

Is my Autosweep/Easytrip login required?
No - the app only needs the account number you're tracking, not a login.

Is my data backed up anywhere?
No. Your saved accounts and balance history live only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them permanently.
