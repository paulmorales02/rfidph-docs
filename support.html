Support - RFID PH

RFID PH shows your Autosweep and Easytrip toll balances together in one app, so you don't have to check two provider websites separately. This page covers common questions and how to reach us.

Does the app need my Autosweep or Easytrip login?

Yes. Those providers don't offer a public way to look up a balance without signing in, so RFID PH signs in to their customer portal on your behalf to read it. You connect your own Autosweep and/or Easytrip account - the same username and password you already use on that provider's own website. RFID PH has no accounts of its own and there is no sign-up with us.

Your password is stored only in your device's secure system keychain, is never sent to us or anyone other than that provider, and is never synced to iCloud or any cloud backup. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

I don't have an account with Autosweep or Easytrip yet

You'll need one to check a balance, since the balance lives with them, not with us. The app links you directly to each provider's own registration page.

How do I add an account?

Tap the + button on the Autosweep or Easytrip row of the Summary card. The first time you add an account for a provider, the app asks you to sign in to that provider and checks the login right away - if the username or password is wrong, it tells you immediately rather than saving it. Then give the account a nickname, enter its account number, and tap "Check & Save".

Once a provider is connected, that saved login is reused for any further accounts you add under the same provider, so you only sign in once per provider.

How do I disconnect a provider?

Tap the account icon in the top-left of the home screen to open Connected Accounts, then tap Disconnect next to that provider. Its saved login is removed from your device immediately. Your tracked accounts and their balance history stay - you just won't be able to refresh them until you reconnect.

How do I refresh a balance?

Tap the refresh icon on any account card to check just that one, or pull down on the home screen to refresh everything at once. There's a short cooldown of about ten seconds after a successful refresh of the same account, so the provider's site isn't hit repeatedly in quick succession.

Why is a lookup sometimes slow, or says the server is busy?

Balances are fetched live from each provider's own site, so lookup speed depends on how quickly that provider responds. Autosweep in particular can occasionally take up to a minute, or report itself as busy - if that happens, waiting a bit and trying again usually works.

Can I remove an account, or clear its history?

Tap the delete icon on an account card to remove it entirely, along with its balance history. To keep the account but clear only its recorded history, open the account and tap the clear-history icon at the top of that screen.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App Lock in Settings and the app will ask for your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode when you open it. RFID PH doesn't create or store a PIN of its own - it uses your device's own authentication.

Is RFID PH affiliated with Autosweep or Easytrip?

No. RFID PH is an independent utility app and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by either provider. Balances are retrieved from each provider's own customer portal and reflect what those portals report at the time of checking.
